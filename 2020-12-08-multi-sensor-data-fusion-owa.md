This paper proposes a hybrid method for fusing uncertain and conflicting evidence within Dempster–Shafer theory (DST). It first measures the credibility of each evidence source using the pignistic probability distance, then applies a soft likelihood function (SLF) based on ordered weighted averaging (OWA) operators to combine compatible probability information before invoking Dempster's combination rule. By accounting for both single-source and combined-source credibility and down-weighting unreliable ("fraudulent") evidence, the approach resolves highly conflicting evidence more reliably than existing conflict-management methods. On a multi-sensor target recognition task, it reaches a recognition rate of 96.92% when fusing three pieces of evidence and 100% when fusing four.

*Second author. Earlier work on information fusion at Northwest A&F University.*
