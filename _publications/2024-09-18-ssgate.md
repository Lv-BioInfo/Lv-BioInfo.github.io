SSGATE integrates transcriptomics and proteomics for both single-cell and spatially resolved data. It constructs neighborhood graphs from single-cell expression profiles or from spatial coordinates, then trains two graph attention auto-encoders along separate paths through self-supervised learning, using a combined weighted loss to preserve both shared and modality-specific information. Across CITE-seq and Stereo-CITE-seq datasets from different tissues, SSGATE outperforms competitive methods in accuracy and robustness and facilitates downstream analyses such as cell clustering and developmental trajectory inference.
